# Course Schedule

The schedule identifies the chapter and coverage for each week. A chapter guide may
be used for more than one week; continue with the coverage listed in the table instead
of looking for a separate weekly document.

| Week | Date | Topic, material, and coverage |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | 2026-09-10 | **Course Introduction and Relational Structure**<br>Material: [Ch2](chapters/ch02/README.md)<br>Coverage: Course expectations; relations, tuples, attributes, domains, schemas, instances, and keys. |
| 2 | 2026-09-17 | **Keys and Relational Algebra**<br>Material: [Ch2](chapters/ch02/README.md)<br>Coverage: Foreign keys, schema diagrams, selection, projection, product, join, and set operations. |
| 3 | 2026-09-24 | **Basic SQL**<br>Material: [Ch3](chapters/ch03/README.md)<br>Coverage: DDL basics; SELECT, FROM, WHERE, aliases, expressions, duplicates, patterns, ordering, and set operations. |
| 4 | 2026-10-01 | **NULL, Aggregation, and Selected Subqueries**<br>Material: [Ch3](chapters/ch03/README.md)<br>Coverage: Three-valued logic, aggregation, GROUP BY, HAVING, selected subqueries, CTEs, and reversible modifications. |
| 5 | 2026-10-08 | **Intermediate SQL**<br>Material: [Ch4](chapters/ch04/README.md)<br>Coverage: Explicit and outer joins, ON and WHERE, views, constraints, reference actions, and basic transactions. |
| 6 | 2026-10-15 | **Written Exam 1**<br>Material: [Ch2](chapters/ch02/README.md), [Ch3](chapters/ch03/README.md), [Ch4](chapters/ch04/README.md)<br>Coverage: Written Exam 1 on the selected classroom material from Chapters 2-4. |

| 10 | 2026-11-12 | **E-R Design and Relational Mapping**<br>Material: [Ch6](chapters/ch06/README.md)<br>Coverage: Redundancy, design choices, E-R-to-relational mapping, keys, foreign keys, and mapped-schema verification. |
